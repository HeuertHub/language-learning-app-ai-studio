## Section 1: Cyrillic Script Literacy & Primary Vowel Systems (Монгол кирилл цагаан толгой ба үндсэн эгшиг)
- **CEFR Level:** Pre-A1 | **Units:** 7 units
- **Communicative Purpose:** Enable the learner to read isolated Cyrillic characters, identify own name in Cyrillic, sound out simple one-syllable words, and operate a standard Mongolian Cyrillic keyboard.
- **Grammar Scope:** Pre-morphological foundational stage: letter-sound correspondence, distinction between vowels and consonants, basic word boundaries.
- **Phonology & Orthography:** 35-letter Cyrillic inventory, 7 cardinal short vowels [a, i, ɔ, ʊ, o, u, e], non-Russian unique vowels Ө [o] and Ү [u], Latin-Cyrillic false friends (В, Н, Р, Х, У, С).
- **Cultural Context:** Historical adoption of the 35-letter Cyrillic script in 1941 designed by Ts. Damdinsüren, its coexistence with traditional Mongolian script, and modern digital keyboard layout conventions in Ulaanbaatar.

## Section 2: Syllable Structure, Vowel Harmony & Emergent Survival Formulae (Үеийн бүтэц, эгшиг зохицох ёс ба анхан шатны хэллэг)
- **CEFR Level:** Pre-A1 | **Units:** 8 units
- **Communicative Purpose:** Enable learners to greet peers and elders politely, introduce their own name, express simple thanks, and read signs displaying common names, public signs, and digits 1-10.
- **Grammar Scope:** Introduction to root vowel harmony constraints (эр/эм эгшиг), initial concept of the zero-copula identity sentence frame, and uninflected nominative noun usage.
- **Phonology & Orthography:** Long vowel double-spelling (аа, оо, уу, ээ, өө, үү, ий), diphthongs (ай, ой, уй, үй, эй), iotated vowels (я, е, ё, ю), basic back/front masculine/feminine vowel harmony (эр, эм эгшиг).
- **Cultural Context:** Social sanctity of greetings in Mongolian culture; eye contact, gentle handshake or nod, and the unacceptability of skipping greetings when entering a room or physical ger.

## Section 3: Identity, Courtesy & Nominal Equational Clauses (Танилцах, мэндлэх ба нэрийн өгүүлбэр)

- **Communicative Purpose:** Introduce oneself and others, state nationality, profession, exchange elder salutations, apologize, and ask basic yes/no identity questions.
- **Grammar Scope:** Zero-copula nominal sentences, demonstrative pronouns (энэ/тэр/эдгээр/тэдгээр), polar question particles (уу/үү/юу/юү), negative nominal copula (биш), personal pronouns nominative (би, чи, та, тэр, бид, та нар, тэд), unmarked nominative case.
- **Phonology & Orthography:** Fourfold vowel harmony in question particles (уу/үү vs юу/юү after vowels), soft and hard signs (ь, ъ) in personal names.
- **Cultural Context:** Naming traditions in Mongolia (patronymic + given name), historical absence of traditional Western surnames, respectful address protocols in contemporary urban and rural life.
